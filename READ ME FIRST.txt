HOW TO OPEN
Unzip the folder, then open "index.html" (the brief) in any browser
(Chrome recommended, 1440px wide or more). Every file is self-contained and works
offline — no install, plugins or internet connection needed. Keep all files in the
same folder so the links between documents work.

Documents 3, 4, 5 and the appendices are wide canvases. Each one has a zoom
control in the bottom left corner: press Fit to pull the whole board into view,
then zoom back in on whatever you want to read. You can also pinch to zoom
(or Ctrl/Cmd + scroll), hold Space and drag to pan, and press F to fit.
